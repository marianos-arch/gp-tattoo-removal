Qualifications

Only visible and offensive/gang-related tattoos are eligible for tattoo removal.

Visible Tattoos Definition:

Face, neck, shoulder to fingers, knees to feet.

  • Tattoo removal patients that have already started removal of tattoos that don’t meet the above definition can still continue getting those tattoos removed.
  • Please present a valid ID for your first tattoo removal appointment. For minors, parents may submit their own ID instead.

How it Works

During a treatment, our Astanza Duality laser sends multiple pulses of light energy onto the unwanted tattoo. The light energy safely penetrates through the upper layers of skin and targets the tattoo ink particles located in the dermis, the skin’s deepest layer. The laser selectively targets the unwanted ink particles without damaging any of the surrounding skin tissue, making laser tattoo removal the safest method for removing a tattoo.

The ink particles absorb the light energy, heat up, and shatter into tiny fragmented particles. Over the weeks following a treatment, the immune system sends bacteria-eating cells to absorb the shattered ink particles and naturally flush them out through the lymphatic system.
